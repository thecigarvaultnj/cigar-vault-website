Returns & Refunds

✦

Last updated August 2026

Cigars are perishable, so most sales are final — but we stand behind what we send. Here’s how returns, damage, and refunds work.

Cigar Sales Are Final

Because tobacco is perishable and we can’t resell a product once it has left our humidor and traveled to you, all cigar sales are final except in the cases below.

Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Orders

If your order shows up damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery and include photographs of the product and the packaging. We’ll review it, and if we approve the claim we’ll either send a replacement or issue a full refund — our choice, based on what’s in stock. The photos help us make it right quickly and fix whatever went wrong on our end or with the carrier.

Accessories and Merch

Unopened accessories and merch can be returned within 14 days of delivery, in their original, unused condition. The buyer pays return shipping. Contact us for authorization before you send anything back — returns sent without authorization can’t be processed.

What We Can’t Take Back

We can’t accept returns on opened cigar boxes or on partially smoked cigars. Once a box is opened or a cigar is lit, it’s yours.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of approval. Shipping charges aren’t refundable unless the mistake was ours.

Subscriptions

Refunds and cancellations for the Key Keepers club are handled under our Terms & Conditions — see the Subscriptions section there.

Before You Dispute a Charge

If something’s wrong, please contact us before you file a dispute with your card company. Almost everything is faster to fix directly, and we want the chance to make it right. Email Thecigarvaultnj@gmail.com or call (973) 333-7475.
