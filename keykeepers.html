Key Keepers

✦

Five cigars a month. Every one of them an unlock you’ve already seen.

Every day we open the vault and put up a Cigar of the Day — one cigar, its story, and why it earned the spotlight. At the end of each month we look back at every unlock, pull the five best, box them together, and send that box to our Key Keepers. No mystery filler, no clearing out the shelf. Just the five cigars that earned their spot that month.

We’re still building it — the box, the cadence, and the way keys are handed out are all being finalized right now. Add your name to the waitlist and you’ll be first to hear the moment the vault opens for members.

New unlocks go up daily on Instagram at @thecigarvaultnj — that’s where the five come from.
