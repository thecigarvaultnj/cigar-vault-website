Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

✦
Do you have a lounge?

Not at the moment. We’re planning to submit a formal request to the Town of Montville for lounge approval, but the process can take time. For now, The Cigar Vault is a retail destination — though we’re working toward becoming a true gathering place for the local cigar community down the road.

Do you have a rewards program?

Not yet, but it’s in the works. We’re putting together a customer appreciation program that we expect to launch within the next 3 months. We want to make sure it’s something genuinely worthwhile for our regulars before rolling it out — stay tuned.

Do you sell cigarettes or electronic vapes?

No — and that’s by design. The Cigar Vault is committed exclusively to premium tobacco. We carry hand-rolled cigars and a curated selection of accessories. You won’t find cigarettes, e-cigarettes, vapes, or disposable nicotine products here. Just quality cigars, the way they’re meant to be enjoyed.
